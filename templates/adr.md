## Decision

One explicit decision and its boundaries. If undecided, identify the proposed choice and keep status proposed. Record decision owner, decision date, and source of authorization for accepted/rejected decisions.

## Rationale

Why the choice meets the drivers, what evidence supports it, and what uncertainty remains. Reference the RFC rather than duplicating a long investigation.

## Consequences

Positive outcomes, drawbacks, obligations, residual risk, and operational implications. A decision does not by itself close a risk.

## Validation and follow-up

Evidence that will validate the decision, risk closure criterion, linked enablers/stories, dependencies, and review trigger.

## Supersession

Supersedes and superseded-by links, or explicit none. Preserve the history of accepted decisions and explain any supersession.

## Open questions

Unresolved inputs or evidence needed; proposed decisions may remain useful drafts.
