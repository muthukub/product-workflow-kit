## Research brief

Restate the idea in plain English, the working customer/geography, supplied decisions, founder constraints and any material scope assumptions. Record research date and mode: live, partial or unavailable. Follow workflow/LEAN-CANVAS-RESEARCH.md. A short idea is sufficient; do not require a business-school-style brief.

## Problem

Top three concrete problems, who experiences them, and existing alternatives including manual workarounds or doing nothing. Support external facts with direct page links. Label evidence, inferences and hypotheses; qualify anecdotal problem reports.

## Customer segments

Recommend a focused starting segment, separate users from buyers, identify early adopters and exclusions, and explain the evidence or inference behind this choice. Keep an unconfirmed segment/geography visibly provisional.

## Unique value proposition

Propose one clear promise, differentiated outcome and high-level concept. Explain why it may matter against researched alternatives; do not claim uniqueness or superiority without support.

## Solution

Recommend minimal capabilities mapped to the problems and relevant constraints. Mark the solution as a hypothesis, not proven product-market fit.

## Channels

Recommend one or two specific, founder-executable paths for reaching early adopters. Link evidence that the chosen segment can be reached there; label expected conversion and acquisition economics as untested.

## Revenue streams

Recommend who pays, the revenue model, billing unit/frequency and an initial price or price range to test. Compare with sourced advertised competitor prices where available, including currency, tier and date; state quote-only or unavailable pricing honestly. The proposed price and willingness to pay remain hypotheses unless directly validated.

## Cost structure

Identify fixed/variable costs for the minimal solution. Provide a sourced range or a labeled operating scenario with workload, currency, units, arithmetic, date and exclusions. Mark actual founder/private costs unknown where needed. Avoid invented measured economics.
